# Landing Page Architect

Build a landing page that communicates value quickly, earns trust honestly, answers objections, and makes the next step obvious.

## Before layout
1. Identify target audience, traffic source, awareness level, offer, differentiator, and primary conversion.
2. Gather approved product facts, features, pricing, proof, constraints, and brand voice.
3. Identify missing proof or content. Do not fill gaps with fabricated metrics, testimonials, customer logos, guarantees, or claims.
4. Define one primary CTA and any genuinely useful secondary action.

## Recommended narrative (adapt, do not blindly follow)
- Hero: clear outcome/value proposition, audience relevance, supporting explanation, primary CTA, and meaningful visual.
- Credibility: real proof, credentials, customer evidence, or transparent explanation of how the service works.
- Problem/context: demonstrate understanding without exaggerated fear.
- Solution: explain the offer and why it is appropriate.
- Benefits/features: prioritize user outcomes; show features in context.
- Process/how it works: reduce uncertainty and explain next steps.
- Use cases or audience fit: help visitors self-identify.
- Objection handling: pricing, security, compatibility, delivery, support, or FAQs as relevant.
- Final CTA: restate the value and next step.
- Footer: useful navigation, contact details, legal links, and social links only when real.

## Messaging
- Make the hero specific. Avoid vague phrases such as "Transform your future" without concrete context.
- Prefer concise, scannable copy with meaningful headings.
- Keep claims proportional to available evidence.
- Use concrete outcomes and examples where verified.
- Make CTA labels action-oriented and accurate (e.g., "Book a consultation" only if booking exists).

## Visual structure
- Establish a distinct hero composition appropriate to the brand and offer.
- Vary section rhythm while maintaining a coherent grid and spacing system.
- Use real product UI, product imagery, diagrams, or editorial imagery when they explain value.
- Avoid repeating the same card grid in every section.
- Make the page readable and persuasive on mobile.

## Conversion integrity
- Forms must submit to a real configured destination or clearly explain that they are not connected; never imply a successful submission when none occurred.
- Links and buttons must work.
- Do not add fake countdowns, fake stock levels, fake reviews, fake client logos, or manipulative dark patterns.
- Ensure privacy and consent notices are present when collecting personal data, as appropriate.

## Handoff and QA
Deliver section order, message hierarchy, CTA map, required assets, and content gaps. Verify every CTA, form, navigation item, responsive state, and footer link with `visual-qa`.
