# Visual Research & Continuous Improvement Protocol

## Approved research surfaces
- Mobbin: real product UI patterns and flows.
- Awwwards: curated web experiences and art direction.
- Dribbble: visual exploration and concepts; distinguish concepts from shipped products.
- Behance: case studies and identity systems; verify whether details are implemented.
- Real company websites: live messaging, page structure, and production behavior.
- Current design trends: use dated, attributable sources and prefer primary/credible documentation.

## Research record template
For each reference, record:
- Reference title and URL.
- Date inspected.
- Page/screen inspected.
- What was directly observed.
- Useful design principle.
- Why it fits this project.
- What must not be copied.
- Confidence and limitations.

## Trend adoption gate
A trend is adopted only if it improves at least one of:
- user comprehension;
- task completion;
- brand differentiation;
- content hierarchy;
- interaction feedback;
- accessibility or performance.

Assess implementation complexity, maintenance cost, mobile support, reduced-motion behavior, and longevity. Record rejected trends and reasons when useful.

## Research cadence
Research is performed when a relevant project begins, when the user requests trend research, or during an explicitly requested library review. This package does not provide an always-on crawler or background monitor.
