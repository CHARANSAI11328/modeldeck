# Benchmark Brief 03 — Creative Agency

Create a premium landing page for a fictional digital studio called **Form & Function Studio**.

## Audience and goal
Founders and marketing leaders seeking a partner for brand-led websites and digital products. Primary conversion: start a project enquiry.

## Approved facts
- Services: strategy, visual identity, website design, frontend development, and ongoing optimization.
- Studio personality: thoughtful, inventive, precise, collaborative.
- No named clients, case-study outcomes, awards, testimonials, or years-in-business are provided.

## Required sections
Distinctive hero, studio positioning, services, process, selected-work presentation using clearly fictional concept projects, FAQ, project enquiry CTA, footer.

## Design challenge
Create a strong studio identity and memorable composition without copying a specific agency site. Show craft and confidence without fabricated social proof.

## Functional requirements
Working navigation, responsive menu, accessible enquiry form with honest submission behavior, functional links, and complete mobile layout.
