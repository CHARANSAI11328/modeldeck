# Benchmark Brief 02 — Luxury / E-commerce

Create a premium brand landing page for a fictional independent fragrance house called **Atelier Serein**.

## Audience and goal
Design-conscious customers exploring a small-batch fragrance collection. Primary conversion: explore the collection and proceed to a product detail/purchase path.

## Approved facts
- The brand offers a fictional collection of three fragrances.
- Product names: Still / No. 01, Dusk / No. 02, and Solace / No. 03.
- Brand positioning: considered, restrained, sensory, small-batch.
- No real ingredients, certifications, awards, customer reviews, stock levels, or shipping promises are supplied.

## Required sections
Editorial hero, brand philosophy, collection preview, craft/process storytelling, fragrance selection guidance, FAQ, collection CTA, footer.

## Design challenge
Use refined editorial composition, art-directed product imagery or clearly labeled conceptual placeholders, strong typography, and a restrained palette. Avoid fake luxury clichés and fabricated product claims.

## Functional requirements
Responsive navigation, working collection links, accessible product selection interactions if included, and no fake checkout or payment flow.
