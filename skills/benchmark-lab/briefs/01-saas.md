# Benchmark Brief 01 — Technology / SaaS

Create a premium landing page for a fictional workflow analytics SaaS called **Northstar Flow**.

## Audience and goal
Operations leads at growing service businesses. Primary conversion: request a product demo.

## Approved facts
- The product brings task status, workload visibility, and operational reporting into one workspace.
- It offers configurable dashboards, team workload views, and weekly summaries.
- No customer count, revenue impact, certifications, testimonials, or named customers are provided.

## Required sections
Hero, product overview, three capability areas, how it works, use cases, FAQ, demo CTA, footer.

## Design challenge
Communicate clarity, confidence, and operational control. Avoid generic gradient SaaS clichés. Product visuals may be illustrative but must be labeled as illustrative rather than presented as verified screenshots.

## Functional requirements
Working navigation, responsive menu, functional demo-request form with clearly documented local/demo handling if no backend is configured, valid links, keyboard support, and visible form feedback.

## Constraints
Do not invent metrics, customer logos, security certifications, integrations, or pricing.
