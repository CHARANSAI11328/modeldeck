# UI/UX Master

Design interfaces around user goals, clear information architecture, and predictable interaction—not visual decoration alone.

## Discovery
- Identify primary users, their context, top tasks, pain points, and expected outcomes.
- Identify the primary business goal and user success action.
- Inspect existing routes, navigation, content, analytics if available, and established interaction patterns.
- Separate known facts from assumptions. Do not invent research findings or personas and present them as validated.

## Information architecture
1. Group content by user intent and mental model.
2. Establish page hierarchy and navigation labels using plain language.
3. Keep primary actions visually and semantically clear.
4. Avoid unnecessary navigation depth and competing CTAs.
5. Map critical journeys from entry point to successful completion.
6. Include empty, loading, error, success, disabled, and permission states where relevant.

## Layout and hierarchy
- Use a consistent grid, alignment system, spacing rhythm, and readable line lengths.
- Put the most important information first.
- Use size, weight, contrast, spacing, and position to express hierarchy; do not rely on color alone.
- Prefer clear section boundaries and meaningful grouping over excessive cards and borders.
- Ensure headings describe the content that follows.
- Design responsive behavior intentionally, not as a desktop layout squeezed down.

## Interaction design
- Every interactive element must communicate affordance and state.
- Use familiar patterns unless a novel interaction provides a clear benefit.
- Provide visible focus, validation feedback, success/error messages, and clear recovery paths.
- Confirm destructive actions and preserve user input when errors occur.
- Avoid hover-only functionality and inaccessible custom controls.
- Ensure forms have labels, instructions, validation, and appropriate input types.

## Conversion and trust
- Match CTA language to user intent and commitment level.
- Place proof near claims, but use only supplied or verifiable proof.
- Avoid fabricated testimonials, ratings, customer logos, statistics, certifications, or scarcity.
- Reduce friction without hiding pricing, limitations, or important terms.

## Deliverables
Create a sitemap/section map, critical user-flow notes, hierarchy rules, interaction-state inventory, and responsive behavior notes. Coordinate page storytelling with `landing-page-architect`, tokens with `design-systems`, and verification with `accessibility-usability` and `visual-qa`.
